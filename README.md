2. Open the downloaded `.apk` file on your Android device.
3. If prompted by Android, allow **"Install from unknown sources"** for your browser or file manager.
4. Tap **Install** and launch the app!

## ✨ Key Features

- **🏛️ Multi-Model Deliberation Council**: Synthesizes verified rulings across classical jurisprudence, tafsir, and hadith.
- **📚 Dual-Source Engine**: High-fidelity Turath digital folios and the OpenITI classical Arabic corpus.
- **🌐 17 Languages Supported**: Full UI and research presets in Arabic, English, Bengali, Urdu, Turkish, Indonesian, Persian, French, and more.
- **🎙️ Voice Research & Vocal Answers**: Multilingual voice dictation and text-to-speech audio playback across 17 languages.
- **⚡ Offline-First Architecture**: Folio caching, local SQLite storage, and instant multilingual Table of Contents.
